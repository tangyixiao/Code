# P16937 时间之书

## 题目背景

:::info[题目背景]

## 题目描述

「时间之书」的术式是一个巨大的，不断变化的等边三角形。为了方便，可以建立笛卡尔坐标系，则在坐标系中的等边三角形顶点坐标分别为 $(-1, 0), (1, 0), (0, \sqrt{3})$，初始时，该三角形的术式编号为 $1$，且每个顶点的时间权重均为 $1$。

「时间之书」的术式需要进行 $q$ 次操作才能正常发动，每次操作形如：

- `query i` 代表查询术式编号为 $i$ 的三角形三个顶点的时间权重和，芙莉莲需要向其中输送恰好为权重和的魔力才能正确激活术式；
- `update i y` 代表将术式编号为 $i$ 的三角形的三个顶点的时间权重都改为 $y$；
- `extend i` 代表将术式编号为 $i$ 的三角形扩展为四个子三角形，具体地，我们定义：
   - $AB$ 为术式编号为 $i$ 的三角形与 $x$ 轴平行的一条边，且 $A$ 的横坐标小于 $B$ 的横坐标；
   - $C$ 为术式编号为 $i$ 的三角形的第三个顶点，若 $C$ 的纵坐标大于 $A$ 与 $B$ 的纵坐标，则三角形 $i$ 为**正三角形**，否则为**倒三角形**；
   - 设 $AB$ 的中点为 $D$，$AC$ 的中点为 $E$，$BC$ 的中点为 $F$，则对于**正三角形**而言，扩展出的四个子三角形分别是 $\triangle ADE, \triangle DBF, \triangle DEF, \triangle CEF$，它们的术式编号分别为 $4i, 4i+1, 4i+2, 4i+3$；对于**倒三角形**而言，扩展出的四个子三角形分别是 $\triangle DBF, \triangle ADE, \triangle DEF, \triangle CEF$，它们的术式编号分别为 $4i, 4i+1, 4i+2, 4i+3$；
    - 扩展后，若子三角形的顶点已有时间权重，则权重不变，否则权重为 $1$；
    - 保证扩展后的子三角形的术式编号小于 $10^9$。

## 输入格式

输入第一行包含一个整数 $q(1\leq q\leq 10^5)$，表示「时间之书」的术式变换次数。

以下 $q$ 行，每行代表一个操作：

- `query i` 代表查询术式编号为 $i$ 的三角形三个顶点的时间权重和；
- `update i y` 代表将术式编号为 $i$ 的三角形的三个顶点的时间权重都改为 $y(1\leq y\leq 10^9)$；
- `extend i` 代表将术式编号为 $i$ 的三角形扩展为四个子三角形，定义如上。

输入保证所有操作的术式编号均是合法且存在的三角形术式编号。

## 题目描述

The spell pattern of the "Book of Time" is a huge, constantly changing equilateral triangle. For convenience, we build a Cartesian coordinate system. The coordinates of the vertices of the equilateral triangle in the coordinate system are $(-1, 0)$, $(1, 0)$, and $(0, \sqrt{3})$. Initially, the spell ID of this triangle is $1$, and the time weight at each vertex is $1$.

The spell pattern of the "Book of Time" needs to undergo $q$ operations to be activated normally. Each operation is one of the following:

- `query i` means querying the sum of the time weights of the three vertices of the triangle whose spell ID is $i$. Frieren must feed exactly that amount of magic (equal to the sum of weights) to correctly activate the spell;
- `update i y` means changing the time weights of all three vertices of the triangle whose spell ID is $i$ to $y$;
- `extend i` means extending the triangle whose spell ID is $i$ into four child triangles. Specifically, we define:
  - $AB$ is the side of triangle $i$ that is parallel to the $x$ axis, and the $x$ coordinate of $A$ is less than the $x$ coordinate of $B$;
  - $C$ is the third vertex of triangle $i$. If the $y$ coordinate of $C$ is greater than the $y$ coordinates of $A$ and $B$, then triangle $i$ is an **upright triangle**; otherwise it is an **inverted triangle**;
  - Let the midpoint of $AB$ be $D$, the midpoint of $AC$ be $E$, and the midpoint of $BC$ be $F$. Then for an **upright triangle**, the four child triangles are $\triangle ADE, \triangle DBF, \triangle DEF, \triangle CEF$, and their spell IDs are $4i, 4i+1, 4i+2, 4i+3$ respectively. For an **inverted triangle**, the four child triangles are $\triangle DBF, \triangle ADE, \triangle DEF, \triangle CEF$, and their spell IDs are $4i, 4i+1, 4i+2, 4i+3$ respectively;
  - After extension, if a vertex of a child triangle already has a time weight, the weight remains unchanged; otherwise its weight is $1$;
  - It is guaranteed that the spell IDs of the child triangles after extension are less than $10^9$.

For each `query i` operation, Frieren needs to know the sum of the time weights of the three vertices of the triangle whose spell ID is $i$.
