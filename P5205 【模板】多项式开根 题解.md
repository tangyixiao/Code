# P5205 【模板】多项式开根 题解

Since $a_0=1$, the required smaller constant coefficient is $b_0=1$. Suppose $B$ is correct modulo $x^k$. Newton iteration for the equation $B^2-A=0$ gives

$$B\leftarrow\frac{B+A/B}{2}\pmod{x^{2k}}.$$

Compute the polynomial inverse and products with NTT, doubling the known precision each round. The total time complexity is $O(n\log n)$ and the space complexity is $O(n)$.
