# P5050 【模板】多项式多点求值 题解

Build the product tree whose leaf for point $a_i$ is $x-a_i$. Reduce $f$ modulo the polynomial at the root, then recursively reduce each remainder modulo the two child polynomials. At a leaf the constant remainder is exactly the requested value.

Polynomial multiplication and the reversed-polynomial division formula are implemented with NTT. The product tree has $O((n+m)\log m)$ stored coefficients. The total time is $O((n+m)\log^2(n+m))$ and the space usage is $O((n+m)\log m)$.
