# P5158 【模板】多项式快速插值 题解

Let $M(x)=\prod_i(x-x_i)$. Build all subproducts with a product tree, differentiate the root polynomial, and evaluate $M'(x)$ at all $x_i$ with the multipoint-evaluation algorithm. The Lagrange weight at leaf $i$ is

$$w_i=\frac{y_i}{M'(x_i)}.$$

When merging two tree nodes, combine their interpolation polynomials as $F=F_LM_R+F_RM_L$. NTT performs every multiplication. The time complexity is $O(n\log^2 n)$ and the space complexity is $O(n\log n)$.
